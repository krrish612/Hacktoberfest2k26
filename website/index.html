(Selected features 04) 2026
01

Gear passport

One record per body, lens or flash. Make, model, category, a masked serial, purchase details and warranty. The whole identity of a piece of kit on a single page.

Records
02

Read a receipt

Pick a PDF, JPEG or PNG. A model running on your own machine suggests the seller, date, item, price and serial. Every value arrives as a suggestion you review before it is saved.

Local AI
03

Evidence & timeline

Attach the receipt, the warranty document and a photo of the serial. Keep a timeline of purchase, inspections, repairs and transfers so the story of the gear stays in order.

Proof
04

Back up & restore

Copy the database and every original into a plain folder you choose. Ordinary files you can move to another drive, not a locked-in vault.

Safety

Everything stays on the machine. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry, and no network calls except to a local model server you run yourself.

Install the model

Pull a small vision model once with Ollama. It runs on your computer, on 127.0.0.1. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

02 →

Choose a receipt

The app checks the local model first and tells you exactly what is missing if it is not ready. Then it reads the file.

03 →

Review, then save

Each field shows a confidence bar in plain words: “Looks clear”, “Check this”, “Unsure”. Nothing saves until you say so.

04 →

Send the packet

Purchase proof, warranty terms and the serial photo, gathered against one piece of kit, ready for a repair.

(Privacy model) By design

Your gear, your warranty,
your receipts. Your machine.

  • 01

    Records and originals live in the app's private data directory on this device.

  • 02

    Receipt reading uses a model on 127.0.0.1 only, never a remote service.

  • 03

    The interface never touches your files or database directly. Every action goes through a validated command.

  • 04

    Warranty dates are never filled in automatically. You enter and confirm them yourself.
